## Summary

Week 9 NFL injury news dominates, with key players like Tyler Huntley and Deshaun Watson facing uncertainty. Iowa and Nebraska election results top local news, and Midwest weather calls for rain and cooler temps.

## Fantasy Football (Week 9)

• Tyler Huntley (Ravens QB) – season-ending turf toe – Huntley reportedly sustained a season-ending toe injury, per Adam Schefter of ESPN. With the Ravens on a bye this week, it doesn't affect the immediate lineup, but it is a major blow for dynasty and deeper-league managers. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Deshaun Watson (Browns QB) – concussion protocol – The Browns announced Wednesday that Watson has been placed in the league's concussion protocol. His availability for Week 9 is uncertain. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• James Conner (Cardinals RB) – won't return from IR this week – Cardinals head coach Mike LaFleur said Tuesday that Conner (foot) won't return from injured reserve this week, per Darren Urban of the Cardinals' official site. Conner owners need to keep looking for replacement options. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Kerby Joseph (Lions S) – out for Week 9 – Lions coach Dan Campbell said Tuesday that Joseph (knee) will not play in Week 9 against the Dolphins. Defensive IDP managers should adjust. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• D.J. Reed (Lions CB) – not practicing Wednesday – Reed (hip) is not expected to practice Wednesday, per Nolan Bianchi of The Detroit News. His status for Sunday is up in the air. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Daron Payne (Commanders DT) – placed on injured reserve – Payne (foot) was placed on IR by the Commanders on Tuesday, per Tashan Reed of The Washington Post. A significant loss to the Washington defensive line. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Javon Kinlaw (Commanders DT) – season-ending MCL surgery – Kinlaw underwent season-ending surgery Tuesday to repair his MCL, per Nicki Jhabvala of The Athletic. He is done for 2026. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Bhayshul Tuten (Jaguars RB) – game-time decision Thursday – Jaguars coach Liam Coen said Wednesday that Tuten (ankle/foot) is feeling better, but his status for Thursday's contest versus the Ravens is expected to come down to a game-time decision, per John Shipley of SI.com. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

## Around Siouxland & Iowa

• Pete Ricketts wins U.S. Senate race in Nebraska – Ricketts will serve his first full six-year term in the U.S. Senate, beating Omaha Democrat Preston Love Jr. in what was a 2024 special election. [KTIV](https://www.ktiv.com/2026/11/04/pete-ricketts-claims-victory-over-dan-osborn-us-senate-race/)

• Nebraska Gov. Jim Pillen wins reelection – Pillen has been reelected as Nebraska's governor. [KTIV](https://www.ktiv.com/2026/11/04/nebraska-gov-jim-pillen-wins-reelection/)

• Sarah Trone Garriott defeats Zach Nunn in Iowa's 3rd District – Iowa's 3rd Congressional District will have a Democratic representative as state Sen. Sarah Trone Garriott won the district over Republican incumbent Zach Nunn. [KTIV](https://www.ktiv.com/2026/11/04/democrat-sarah-trone-garriott-defeats-incumbent-zach-nunn-iowas-3rd-district/)

• Christina Bohannan flips Iowa 1st District seat – Democrat Christina Bohannan has won the seat for Iowa's 1st Congressional District. [KTIV](https://www.ktiv.com/2026/11/04/christina-bohannan-flips-iowa-1st-district-seat/)
