## Summary

Fantasy managers heading into Week 11 face a key decision at wide receiver with Brandon Aiyuk listed out, while the bye-week drain hits six teams including Green Bay and Seattle. The Chiefs enter Week 11 at 7-2 after a strong win over Atlanta.

## Fantasy Football (Week 11)

• Brandon Aiyuk listed out for Week 11 – The 49ers receiver remains on the injury report and is listed as Out ahead of Sunday, though his agent said Tuesday that Aiyuk is "100 percent healthy." Start/sit managers should treat him as unavailable until he's cleared to play. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Lamar Jackson on IR (thumb) – The Ravens quarterback is on injured reserve after thumb surgery, with an estimated return of Dec. 27. Fantasy managers need a fill-in at QB for the coming weeks. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Josh Jacobs out for Packers Week 11 – Jacobs (finger) is listed Out for Green Bay's game Nov. 25. He has a realistic chance to return then, but fantasy owners should plan for a replacement at RB this week. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Puka Nacua suspended three games – The Rams receiver is on injured reserve after a three-game suspension for violating the NFL's personal conduct policy, eligible to return Dec. 20. This creates a need at WR for Nacua's fantasy managers. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Drake Maye doubtful (ankle) – The Patriots quarterback is listed Doubtful for Week 11 after coach Mike Vrabel said Monday there was no definitive diagnosis on the ankle injury. Have a backup QB ready. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Chiefs add WR Josh Downs via trade – Kansas City traded for Downs on Tuesday, giving Patrick Mahomes a new target heading into the Cardinals game. Downs is worth a look as a waiver add in deeper leagues. [Chiefs.com](https://www.chiefs.com/news)

• Week 11 byes – Atlanta, Cleveland, Green Bay, Los Angeles Rams, New England, and Seattle are on bye. Managers with players from those teams need full fill-in lineups. [ESPN schedule](https://www.espn.com/nfl/schedule)

## Around Siouxland & Iowa

• Iowa women's basketball rallies past Montana State – The No. 8 Hawkeyes trailed by 10 in the second quarter before Jan Jensen sparked a 20-2 run, and Iowa won 76-60 at Carver-Hawkeye Arena on Thursday. [Sioux City Journal](https://www.siouxcityjournal.com/)

• Woodbury Central wins first football title since 1980 – The Woodbury Central football team beat West Sioux to capture the Class A state championship, the school's second-ever title. [Sioux City Journal](https://www.siouxcityjournal.com/)

• 44 Iowa census tracts approved as Opportunity Zones – The Treasury Department approved 44 tracts in Iowa as Qualified Opportunity Zones, including three in Sioux City, as part of a federal investment program. [Sioux City Journal](https://www.siouxcityjournal.com/)
