## Summary

Saquon Barkley and A.J. Brown are both questionable for Week 8, while Blake Corum and Omar Cooper Jr. look like strong flex plays. Closer to home, South Sioux City voters face a $95M school bond and multiple fire departments battled a house fire Thursday night.

## Fantasy Football (Week 8)

• Saquon Barkley (PHI) questionable for Week 8 – Barkley missed Wednesday and Thursday practice with a hamstring/back issue ahead of Sunday's game against the Commanders. If he sits, Philly would turn to practice-squad back Jaydon Blue and Elijah Mitchell, both risky flex plays. His status is worth monitoring right up to kickoff. [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/fantasy/article/nfl-injury-report-week-8-latest-news-updates-on-lamar-jackson-breece-hall-more-players-for-fantasy-football-170704038.html)

• A.J. Brown (NE) status up in the air – Brown was not at Thursday practice with an ankle/knee issue, and his availability for Week 8 is uncertain. If he sits, New England would lean more on DeMarios Douglas, Efton Chism III, Hunter Henry, and Eli Raridon, with Rhamondre Stevenson likely seeing more looks. [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/fantasy/article/nfl-injury-report-week-8-latest-news-updates-on-lamar-jackson-breece-hall-more-players-for-fantasy-football-170704038.html)

• Stefon Diggs (WAS) set and forget – With Terry McLaurin out for the season, Diggs is the Commanders' only proven wideout and faces an Eagles defense that ranks among the worst versus the slot and struggles to pressure the quarterback. A safe Week 8 starter. [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/fantasy/article/fantasy-football-start-em-sit-em-joel-smyth-helps-with-tough-flex-lineup-decisions-for-week-8-145428348.html)

• Blake Corum (LAR) flex start – Corum has had two straight strong games and the Rams are expected to control the game script against a Chargers defense that has been run-heavy against all season. Corum's touchdown upside looks good in this matchup. [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/fantasy/article/fantasy-football-start-em-sit-em-joel-smyth-helps-with-tough-flex-lineup-decisions-for-week-8-145428348.html)

• Omar Cooper Jr. (NYJ) double flex start – The rookie has two straight 20-point games and faces a Raiders pass defense that ranks 27th in EPA. With the game projected as a toss-up, the Jets should see plenty of passing volume. [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/fantasy/article/fantasy-football-start-em-sit-em-joel-smyth-helps-with-tough-flex-lineup-decisions-for-week-8-145428348.html)

• Bam Knight (ARI) waiver add – Knight has 161 total yards on 27 touches over the last two weeks and draws a Cowboys defense that has been weak against both the run and pass. Listed at 24% rostered on Yahoo. [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/fantasy/article/week-8-fantasy-football-sleepers-2-rookie-receivers-stick-out-as-underrated-options-off-the-waiver-wire-141933244.html)

• Antonio Williams (WAS) waiver add – With McLaurin out and Jacoby Croskey-Merritt missing time, Washington should lean on the pass against an Eagles defense that covers tight ends better than wide receivers. Listed at 19% rostered on Yahoo. [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/fantasy/article/week-8-fantasy-football-sleepers-2-rookie-receivers-stick-out-as-underrated-options-off-the-waiver-wire-141933244.html)

## Around Siouxland & Iowa

• $95M bond for Covington Elementary – South Sioux City district leaders toured the 1924 school Thursday, citing overcrowding, deteriorating basement plumbing, and foundation issues. Voters will decide on the bond next Tuesday. [KTIV](https://www.ktiv.com/2026/10/30/aging-building-overcrowding-drive-push-95m-bond-covington-elementary/)

• South Sioux City house fire – Multiple fire departments from Sergeant Bluff, Sioux City, and Dakota City responded Thursday night to a home near East 10th and B streets. Residents reported smoke and a heat haze; officials believe the issue is electrical and the investigation is active. [KTIV](https://www.ktiv.com/2026/10/30/multiple-fire-departments-scene-south-sioux-city-house-fire/)

• Halloween events across Sioux City – Kids got an early start on trick-or-treating at events including a first-ever Trunk or Treat at UnityPoint Health's PACE Senior Care, where staff decorated car trunks with themes like Toy Story and Jurassic Park. [KTIV](https://www.ktiv.com/2026/10/30/families-enjoy-halloween-early-events-across-sioux-city/)

• Lawton/Sioux City weather outlook – Today: chance of showers, high near 55. Tonight: 50% chance of showers, low around 48. Saturday: 40% chance of showers, high near 63. Sunday: mostly cloudy, high near 64. Showers return Sunday night and Monday. [National Weather Service](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?textField1=42.49&textField2=-96.25)
