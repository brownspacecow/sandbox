## Summary

Micah Parsons is cleared to play for the Cowboys, free-agent wide receiver Brandon Aiyuk is available on waivers, and the Siouxland area gears up for the third annual Tour of Steeples church tour this weekend.

## Fantasy Football (Week 12)

• Micah Parsons cleared to play Week 12 – The Cowboys' star linebacker was a game-time decision with an ankle injury but is now expected to suit up against the Commanders. If you're starting a Cowboys defender or facing Dallas, Parsons' presence changes the matchup math. [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/news/micah-parsons-cowboys-expected-play-222800000.html)

• Brandon Aiyuk released by 49ers, now a free agent – San Francisco cut veteran WR Brandon Aiyuk, making him available to sign with any team. He's a waiver-wire add worth stashing if a contender picks him up, especially in deeper leagues. [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/news/brandon-aiyuk-released-49ers-free-agent-222500000.html)

• Thursday Night Football: Chiefs at Bills – Kansas City travels to Buffalo for a Thanksgiving night showdown. With the weather looking cold and possibly snowy in Buffalo, keep an eye on the passing game for both teams. Mahomes and Kelce remain must-starts, but have a backup plan if the elements slow the Chiefs' offense. [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/nfl/)

• Week 12 byes: Packers, Rams, Lions, Eagles, Cowboys – These five teams are on bye this week, so check your lineup for any players from those rosters and replace them before kickoff. [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/nfl/)

## Around Siouxland & Iowa

• Siouxland churches open doors for third annual Tour of Steeples – The Tour of Steeples returns Dec. 4–5 with 27 participating churches opening their doors for free-will donations that support the Siouxland Soup Kitchen and Warming Shelter. A good weekend outing to see local churches and help the community. [Sioux City Journal](https://siouxcityjournal.com/news/local/article_305e95af-b747-4baf-97d1-6b1ef4072733.html)

• Sioux City officials recognize 24th Annual Memorial March to Honor Lost Children – Mayor Bob Scott read a proclamation at Monday's City Council meeting honoring the annual Memorial March, which remembers children lost to foster care and other circumstances. [Sioux City Journal](https://siouxcityjournal.com/news/local/government-politics/article_39e3e9c9-5221-49dc-a6e1-2a11969b5155.html)

• Midwest weather: cold with a chance of rain/snow this week – The National Weather Service forecasts a high near 36 with cloudy skies and a chance of rain and snow showers today, then partly sunny and 38 on Wednesday. Thanksgiving Day looks sunny with a high near 41. Dress warmly if you're out for the holiday. [National Weather Service](https://api.weather.gov/gridpoints/FSD/112,15/forecast)
