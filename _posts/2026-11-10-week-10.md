## Summary

Puka Nacua is likely headed to injured reserve and Michael Wilson is out for the season, while the NFL trade deadline brought several big moves including Jacoby Brissett to the Ravens and Jeffery Simmons to the Bears -- all key fantasy storylines for Week 10.

## Fantasy Football (Week 10)

• Puka Nacua (WR, Rams) -- Likely headed to IR -- Puka Nacua is likely heading to injured reserve, per ESPN. The Rams' top receiver would miss significant time, leaving fantasy managers scrambling for a replacement wide receiver. [ESPN Fantasy Football](https://www.espn.com/fantasy/football/story/_/id/40699880/fantasy-football-injuries-position-battles-nfl-news-analysis)

• Michael Wilson (WR, Cardinals) -- Out for season -- Michael Wilson is out for the season, leaving the Cardinals thin at receiver. Fantasy managers who rostered him need a waiver-wire replacement for the rest of the year. [ESPN Fantasy Football](https://www.espn.com/fantasy/football/story/_/id/40699880/fantasy-football-injuries-position-battles-nfl-news-analysis)

• Jacoby Brissett (QB) -- Traded to Ravens -- The Cardinals traded QB Jacoby Brissett to the Ravens in exchange for a 2027 fourth-round pick, a 2028 seventh-round pick, and a 2027 fifth-round pick. Brissett could start for Baltimore as early as Week 10 against the Chargers on Monday Night Football, giving Greg a potential streaming option at quarterback. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/50043471/2026-trade-deadline-updates-latest-news-deals-terms)

• Jeffery Simmons (DT) -- Traded to Bears -- The Titans traded All-Pro defensive tackle Jeffery Simmons to the Bears for 2027 and 2028 first-round picks plus a 2027 fourth-rounder. This is a major defensive move that could shake up the Bears' front seven. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/50043471/2026-trade-deadline-updates-latest-news-deals-terms)

• Trade deadline moves -- Tuesday at 4 p.m. ET is the 2026 NFL trade deadline. Already completed: the Jaguars acquired safety Jessie Bates III and defensive tackles Davon Godchaux and Jordan Davis; the Lions added safety Andre Cisco; the Giants got quarterback J.J. McCarthy; and the Cowboys added cornerback Joey Porter Jr. Greg should watch for any last-minute deals before the deadline. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/50043471/2026-trade-deadline-updates-latest-news-deals-terms)

• Joe Burrow and Ja'Marr Chase -- Eric Moody of ESPN notes that Joe Burrow and Ja'Marr Chase are matchup-proof in fantasy. If Greg has them, they are safe starts regardless of the opposing defense. [ESPN Video](https://www.espn.com/video/clip/_/id/50422661/joe-burrow-jamarr-chase-matchup-proof-fantasy)

• Sioux City Public Museum exhibit -- The Sioux City Public Museum is opening a "New to You" exhibit. Worth a visit for Greg if he enjoys local history. [Sioux City Journal](https://www.siouxcityjournal.com)
