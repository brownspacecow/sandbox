## Summary

Malik Willis heads to injured reserve and Puka Nacua likely joins him, while elevated fire danger covers Iowa and the state is drought-free for the first time this fall.

## Fantasy Football (Week 11)

• Malik Willis (Dolphins QB) – injured reserve – Willis was placed on IR Tuesday with a groin injury and is shelved for at least four weeks. Second-year QB Kyle McCord will start Sunday on the road against the Indianapolis Colts. Fantasy managers need a QB replacement. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Puka Nacua (Rams WR) – likely injured reserve – Nacua suffered a turf toe injury and sprained his MCL and PCL in Sunday's 20-13 win over the Commanders, and head coach Sean McVay said Monday he is likely headed to IR. The Rams' top receiver is out long-term. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Audric Estime (Saints RB) – waiver wire add – Injuries and bye weeks have Fantasy backfields in disarray, and Audric Estime is a top waiver-wire option who could provide first aid for managers scrambling at running back. [CBS Sports](https://www.cbssports.com/fantasy/football/news/week-10-waiver-wire-audric-estime-could-be-the-cure/)

## Around Siouxland & Iowa

• Lawton, Iowa weather – The National Weather Service forecasts mostly cloudy skies gradually becoming sunny today with a high near 63°F and breezy south winds. Tonight mostly clear with a low around 37°F. Thursday sunny with a high near 59°F. A 40 percent chance of rain Friday night. [National Weather Service](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lat=42.4697&lon=-96.2551)

• Elevated fire danger in Iowa – Elevated fire danger is in place Wednesday due to warm, dry, and windy conditions. Avoid open burning and outdoor flames. [KTIV](https://www.ktiv.com)

• Iowa is drought free – October rainfall leaves Iowa drought free, per the Iowa DNR's Water Summary Update. Good news for hunters, anglers, and gardeners heading into late fall. [Iowa DNR](https://www.iowadnr.gov/news-release/2026-11-12/water-summary-update-october-rainfall-leaves-iowa-drought-free)

## More for Greg

• Add winter wonder to your yard or acreage – The Iowa DNR offers tips for adding winter interest to your yard with plants that provide color and wildlife value through the colder months. [Iowa DNR](https://www.iowadnr.gov/news-release/2026-11-13/add-winter-wonder-your-yard-or-acreage)

• McKinley Lake fish renovation – McKinley Lake in Creston will have its water level dropped ahead of a fish renovation project and restocking; a public meeting is set for Nov. 19. Of interest to local anglers. [Iowa DNR](https://www.iowadnr.gov/news-release/2026-11-13/mckinley-lake-water-level-drop-ahead-fish-renovation-project)
