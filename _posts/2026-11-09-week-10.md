## Summary

Chiefs running back Isiah Pacheco is headed to injured reserve with a back injury ahead of Kansas City's Week 10 game against the Broncos, and Ravens star Lamar Jackson is on IR after thumb surgery — two big fantasy storylines for Greg's fantasy league this week.

## Fantasy Football (Week 10)

• Isiah Pacheco (RB, Chiefs) – Injured Reserve (back) – Pacheco (back) has been placed on injured reserve and is expected to miss multiple weeks, with a chance to return in late December per reports. Greg's fantasy managers who roster him need a replacement RB for Week 10, especially with the Chiefs facing the Broncos on Monday night. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Lamar Jackson (QB, Ravens) – Injured Reserve (thumb) – Jackson is scheduled to undergo surgery on his injured right thumb and plans to return later this season, per Ian Rapoport of NFL Network. He'll miss Week 10, so Greg should pivot to a backup QB or stream a waiver-wire option. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries) [NFL.com](https://www.nfl.com/injuries)

• David Njoku (TE, Browns) – Injured Reserve (fibula) – Njoku (fibula) was not activated from injured reserve ahead of Sunday's game against the Texans and remains on IR. Fantasy managers needing a tight end should look to the waiver wire. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• George Pickens (WR, Steelers) – Out (hamstring) – Pickens (hamstring) is inactive for Sunday's game at Indianapolis. If Greg has Pickens, he'll need a fill-in WR for Week 10. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Joshua Palmer (WR, Bills) – Out (hamstring) – Palmer (hamstring) has been ruled out for Monday's game in Minnesota. He's a key Bills receiver, so monitor the Buffalo passing game for Week 10 waiver targets. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Sam Darnold (QB, Vikings) – Out (quadriceps) – Darnold (quadriceps) may miss Seattle's Week 10 game at Las Vegas, per Adam Schefter of ESPN. This is flagged as "per reports" and not yet confirmed for Week 10. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)
