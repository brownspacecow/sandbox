## Summary

Patriots quarterback Drake Maye is dealing with an ankle injury after Week 11 action, and several key players are questionable or out for the upcoming slate. Siouxland mourns Rock Valley teacher and coach Brian Moser, while Santa's mailbox arrives in downtown Sioux City and the weather stays mild for Lawton.

## Fantasy Football (Week 11)

• Drake Maye (Patriots QB) ankle injury – Maye injured his ankle on a sack and is undergoing further testing. His status is uncertain; check back for updates before setting your lineup. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/50453290/week-10-predictions-upsets-fantasy-sleepers-stats-2026) [NFL.com](https://www.nfl.com/injuries)

• A.J. Brown (Eagles WR) questionable – Brown is listed as questionable with ankle and knee injuries. If he can't play, the Eagles' passing game takes a hit. [NFL.com](https://www.nfl.com/injuries)

• Micah Parsons (Cowboys LB) out – Parsons is out with a knee and illness. Dallas loses a key pass rusher this week. [NFL.com](https://www.nfl.com/injuries)

• Mike Evans (Buccaneers WR) out – Evans is out with an Achilles injury. Tampa Bay's top receiver is sidelined. [NFL.com](https://www.nfl.com/injuries)

• Garrett Wilson (Jets WR) doubtful – Wilson is doubtful with a concussion. Monitor his status closely before deciding whether to start him. [NFL.com](https://www.nfl.com/injuries)

• Week 11 matchup: Arizona @ Kansas City – The Chiefs (7-2) host the Cardinals on Sunday, November 22 at 1:00 PM on CBS. Kansas City is a 16.5-point favorite. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/schedule/_/week/11) [Chiefs.com](https://www.chiefs.com/)
