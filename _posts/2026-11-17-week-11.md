## Summary

Lamar Jackson and Gardner Minshew are headed to injured reserve, while the Chiefs notched a 35-13 win over the Falcons behind Mahomes' five touchdowns and a breakout game from Emmett Johnson. Lawton faces a breezy, mild week with rain possible by the weekend.

## Fantasy Football (Week 11)

• Lamar Jackson (Ravens QB) – injured reserve – Jackson (thumb) is expected to be out 6-to-10 weeks, per head coach Jesse Minter. Baltimore turns to Jacoby Brissett for the rest of Jackson's absence. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Gardner Minshew (Cardinals QB) – injured reserve – Minshew suffered a torn UCL in his right throwing elbow and is slated for season-ending surgery, ending his season. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Josh Jacobs (Packers RB) – out – Jacobs is out for the Packers' Week 11 game. Green Bay's running game takes a hit without its lead back. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Marvin Harrison Jr. (Texans WR) – out – Harrison (hamstring) was a non-participant in Monday's walkthrough and is out. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Woody Marks (Texans RB) – doubtful – Marks (concussion) missed Monday's walkthrough and is doubtful to play. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Jordan Addison (Vikings WR) – out – Addison will require thumb surgery and will miss a few weeks, per head coach Kevin O'Connell. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Trey Smith (Chiefs RG) – questionable – Smith (concussion) was ruled out for the remainder of the Falcons game and is questionable heading forward. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Baker Mayfield (Buccaneers QB) – questionable – Mayfield's availability for Sunday's game against Detroit is in question due to a foot/ankle injury. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)
