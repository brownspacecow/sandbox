## Fantasy Football (Week 12)

• Derrick Henry (Ravens RB) – questionable, concussion – Ravens running back Derrick Henry was listed as questionable after entering concussion protocol, but was a full participant in Thursday's practice, suggesting he may be available for Week 12. Fantasy owners should monitor his status closely before setting lineups. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Caleb Williams (Bears QB) – questionable – Bears quarterback Caleb Williams was listed as questionable after Thursday's 39-35 win in Detroit. His status for Week 12 remains uncertain. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Brock Purdy (49ers QB) – questionable, ankle – 49ers quarterback Brock Purdy was listed as a non-participant on Thursday's injury report with an ankle injury. His availability for Week 12 is uncertain. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Brock Bowers (Raiders TE) – questionable, ankle – Raiders tight end Brock Bowers remained a limited participant in Thursday's practice with an ankle injury. His Week 12 status is unclear. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• A.J. Brown (Eagles WR) – doubtful, hamstring – Eagles wide receiver A.J. Brown was listed as doubtful with a hamstring injury after being a non-participant on Thursday's practice estimate. He is unlikely to play in Week 12. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Jordan Addison (Vikings WR) – doubtful, thumb – Vikings wide receiver Jordan Addison was listed as doubtful with a thumb injury after being a non-participant on Thursday's injury report. His Week 12 availability is in doubt. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Jalen Royals (Chiefs WR) – questionable, sprained ankle – Chiefs wide receiver Jalen Royals suffered a sprained ankle during Thursday's 13-10 loss to Buffalo, per head coach Andy Reid. His status for the remainder of Week 12 and beyond is not yet confirmed. [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/nfl/articles/bills-vs-chiefs-live-updates-210048591.html)
