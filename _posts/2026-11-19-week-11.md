## Summary

Baltimore Ravens quarterback Lamar Jackson is headed to injured reserve with a thumb injury, and the Kansas City Chiefs added wide receiver Josh Downs in a trade ahead of Week 11.

## Fantasy Football (Week 11)

• Lamar Jackson (Ravens QB) – injured reserve – Jackson suffered a broken and dislocated thumb and is projected to miss 6 to 10 weeks, per head coach John Harbaugh's staff. The Ravens will turn to Jacoby Brissett under center. Fantasy managers who roster Jackson need a quarterback replacement for the rest of the season. [ESPN](https://site.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/football/nfl/injuries)

• Josh Downs (Colts → Chiefs WR) – traded – The Chiefs acquired Downs from the Indianapolis Colts on Tuesday. Downs is a durable slot receiver who has caught at least 55 passes in each of the last three seasons and gives Patrick Mahomes a new target down the stretch. [Chiefs.com](https://www.chiefs.com/news/five-things-to-know-about-new-chiefs-wr-josh-downs)

• Ladd McConkey (Chargers WR) – questionable – McConkey missed the Chargers' last game with a back issue but returned to limited practice Wednesday, making him trending toward playing Sunday against the Jets. Start/sit managers should watch his Friday practice status before locking him in. [ESPN](https://site.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/football/nfl/injuries)

• T.J. Hockenson (Vikings TE) – questionable – Hockenson sat out all of Week 10 practice with wrist and chest injuries and was ruled out of Sunday's game at Green Bay. He is working back this week and is a start/sit gamble for Week 11 depending on how he practices. [ESPN](https://site.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/football/nfl/injuries)

• Tony Adams (Titans S) – concussion protocol – Adams entered the league's concussion protocol during Tennessee's Week 10 loss to Jacksonville and must clear the five-step process before returning. An IDP/defensive lineup note for managers in leagues that use individual defensive players. [ESPN](https://site.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/football/nfl/injuries)

## Around Siouxland & Iowa

• Lawton, Iowa weather – The National Weather Service forecasts sunny skies today with a high near 60°F, then mostly cloudy tonight with a low around 44°F. Friday looks partly sunny and breezy with a high near 66°F, followed by a chance of rain Saturday. [National Weather Service](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lat=42.4697&lon=-96.2551)

• Iowa deer seasons opening – Iowa's two regular gun deer seasons will host more than 100,000 hunters over the first three weekends in December, with seasons getting underway soon. A reminder for hunters to check dates and regulations before heading out. [Iowa DNR](https://www.iowadnr.gov/news-release/2026-11-17/iowas-most-popular-deer-seasons-are-right-around-corner)

• Trout spawning season – Iowa DNR reports trout spawning is in full swing, of interest to anglers and those watching the state's trout streams this fall. [Iowa DNR](https://www.iowadnr.gov/news-release/2026-11-17/trout-spawning-season-full-swing)

## More for Greg

• Kansas City Chiefs – The Chiefs moved to 7-2 with a 35-13 win over the Atlanta Falcons on Sunday, with Patrick Mahomes throwing five touchdowns. Kansas City faces the Arizona Cardinals on Thursday in Week 11. [Chiefs.com](https://www.chiefs.com/news/)
