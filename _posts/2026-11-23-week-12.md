## Summary

Fantasy managers heading into the Week 12 Thanksgiving slate should track several fresh injury and role changes, including the 49ers losing Trent Williams' replacement McKivitz for the season and the Chiefs taking on Buffalo on Friday night.

## Fantasy Football (Week 12)

• Puka Nacua out for the season – The Rams wide receiver underwent MCL surgery and faces a four-to-five-month recovery, meaning he will miss the playoffs and the rest of the 2026 season. Fantasy managers should move on from Nacua and look for a replacement wide receiver. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/50556613/sources-rams-puka-nacua-4-5-months-knee-surgery)

• Roman Wilson activated off IR – The Steelers activated wide receiver Roman Wilson from injured reserve ahead of Sunday's game, giving Pittsburgh's passing game a boost alongside DK Metcalf and Michael Pittman Jr. Wilson is worth a waiver look in deeper leagues. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/50552701/sources-steelers-activate-wr-roman-wilson-ir-eagles-game)

• Colts turn to Anthony Richardson – Indianapolis cut quarterback Daniel Jones and will start Anthony Richardson going forward. Richardson is an immediate waiver add at quarterback in most formats and a stash in deeper leagues. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/50542293/colts-release-qb-daniel-jones-blowout-loss-texans)

• 49ers lose RT McKivitz for season – San Francisco right tackle Colton McKivitz suffered a serious ankle injury against the Vikings and is out for the rest of the 2026 season. This is a blow to the 49ers' offensive line depth heading into the stretch run. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/50563342/week-11-sunday-games-takeaways-questions-2026)

• Week 12 start/sit and matchups – ESPN's lineup advice breaks down the vital insights for the Thanksgiving and Sunday slate before you finalize your starting lineup. Check this before setting your Week 12 roster. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/fantasy/football/story/_/id/50531472/fantasy-football-start-sit-week-11-need-know)

• Chiefs @ Bills on Thanksgiving – Kansas City travels to Buffalo on Friday, November 27 for a prime-time showdown. Greg should keep an eye on Travis Kelce and the Chiefs' passing game in a tough matchup against a strong Bills defense. [ESPN](https://site.api.espn.com/apis/site/v2/sports/football/nfl/scoreboard?week=12&seasontype=2&year=2026)
