## Summary

Puka Nacua is likely headed to injured reserve and Malik Willis is out for the season, while the NFL trade deadline brought major moves including Kirk Cousins to the Vikings and Josh Downs to the Chiefs -- key fantasy storylines heading into Week 11.

## Fantasy Football (Week 11)

• Puka Nacua (WR, Rams) – likely headed to IR – Rams coach Sean McVay said Nacua is likely to be placed on injured reserve after suffering turf toe and a sprained MCL and PCL in Sunday's win over the Commanders. He would miss significant time, leaving fantasy managers scrambling for a replacement wide receiver. [NFL.com](https://www.nfl.com/news/rams-wr-nacua-likely-to-go-on-ir-with-turf-toe-sprained-mcl-and-pcl)

• Malik Willis (QB, Dolphins) – out for the season – Malik Willis was placed on injured reserve with a groin injury, and second-year QB Kyle McCord will make his first start, beginning Sunday on the road against the Indianapolis Colts. [NFL.com](https://www.nfl.com/news/dolphins-place-qb-willis-groin-on-ir-mccord-to-make-first-start)

• Josh Jacobs (RB, Packers) – out this week – Josh Jacobs will undergo surgery on his fractured finger this week, per Packers coach Matt LaFleur, and his timeline to return is "to be determined." [NFL.com](https://www.nfl.com/news/packers-jacobs-to-undergo-surgery-on-finger-this-week-timeline-to-return-tbd)

• Sam Darnold (QB, Seahawks) – out, Drew Lock to start – Seahawks coach Mike Macdonald said Sam Darnold will not play as he recovers from a quad injury, with Drew Lock expected to start again. [NFL.com](https://www.nfl.com/news/darnold-out-for-wk-10-vs-raiders-banged-up-lock-expected-to-start-again)

• Trade deadline recap – The trade deadline passed Tuesday at 4 p.m. ET. Big moves: the Vikings acquired Kirk Cousins from the Raiders; the Chiefs acquired Josh Downs and a 2027 seventh-round pick from the Colts; the 49ers acquired Keenan Allen from the Colts. Greg should watch how these new roles shake up Week 11 lineups. [NFL.com](https://www.nfl.com/news/nfl-trade-deadline-track-every-move-since-start-of-2026-regular-season)

## Around Siouxland & Iowa

• 6th Street bridge in Sioux City reopens – The 6th Street bridge over the Floyd River has reopened to vehicle traffic following an eight-month reconstruction project. Good news for local drivers. [KTIV](https://www.ktiv.com)

• Woodbury County post-election audit – Woodbury County officials hand-counted nearly 2,000 ballots on Tuesday, November 10, as part of a post-election audit. [KTIV](https://www.ktiv.com)

• Veterans Day weather – Veterans Day brings sunshine with a high near 55°F in Lawton, followed by a cool-down with highs in the low 40s through the weekend. [National Weather Service](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lat=42.49&lon=-96.25)

## More for Greg

• Iowa Hawkeyes fall to Virginia Tech in OT at Tyson Events Center – The Iowa Hawkeyes men's basketball team fell 93-88 in overtime to Virginia Tech on Tuesday, November 10, at the Tyson Events Center in Sioux City. A sold-out crowd watched the Division I matchup. [Sioux City Journal](https://www.siouxcityjournal.com)

• Kansas City food drive – The Chiefs kicked off their 12th annual Chiefs Kingdom Food Drive benefiting Harvesters. [Chiefs.com](https://www.chiefs.com)
