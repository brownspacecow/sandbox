## Summary

Week 11 brings new fantasy football injury designations including TreVeyon Henderson on IR and Puka Nacua likely headed to IR, plus Iowa DNR outdoors updates on lake renovation and drought recovery, and a sunny weekend forecast for Lawton before rain arrives late in the week.

## Fantasy Football (Week 11)

• TreVeyon Henderson (Patriots RB) – placed on injured reserve – The rookie running back is out for Week 11 with an illness and has been placed on injured reserve, opening a larger role for Corey Kiner in the New England backfield. Rhamondre Stevenson is listed as questionable with a knee tendon issue. Detroit allows the second-fewest fantasy points per game to running backs, so it is a tough matchup, but Kiner could see 14-plus rushing attempts if Stevenson cannot go. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/50453290/week-10-predictions-upsets-fantasy-sleepers-stats-2026)

• Puka Nacua (Rams WR) – likely headed to injured reserve – The Rams wide receiver is likely to be placed on injured reserve with turf toe, a sprained MCL, and a sprained PCL. His absence could mean more targets for Tutu Atwell and other healthy Rams receivers against the Cardinals. Arizona allows 32.2 fantasy points per game to wide receivers. [NFL.com](https://www.nfl.com/injuries)

• Josh Jacobs (Packers RB) – out for Week 11 – The Packers running back is out with a finger injury and is expected to undergo surgery this week, with his return timeline still to be determined. This creates opportunity for the Packers' backup running backs in the Week 11 slate. [NFL.com](https://www.nfl.com/injuries)

• Rhamondre Stevenson (Patriots RB) – questionable – The Patriots running back is listed as questionable with a knee tendon issue for Week 11 against the Lions. If he cannot play, Corey Kiner would likely take over the lead back role. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/50453290/week-10-predictions-upsets-fantasy-sleepers-stats-2026)

• Kenneth Walker III (Chiefs RB) – to miss Week 11 game – The Chiefs running back (groin) is expected to miss the Week 11 game against the Falcons. The Chiefs will need to adjust their backfield plans with Walker sidelined. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/team/_/name/kc/kansas-city-chiefs)

• Trevor Etienne (Panthers RB) – activated from injured reserve – The Panthers running back has been activated from injured reserve and is back in the passing attack, providing a new option for Carolina's offense in Week 11. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/50453290/week-10-predictions-upsets-fantasy-sleepers-stats-2026)

## Around Siouxland & Iowa

• McKinley Lake water level to drop for fish renovation – The Iowa DNR is lowering McKinley Lake in Creston ahead of a fish renovation project and restocking. A public meeting is set for November 19. [Iowa DNR](https://www.iowadnr.gov/newsroom/news-releases)

• October rainfall leaves Iowa drought free – Iowa had a wetter-than-average October, contributing to ongoing drought recovery across the state, according to the latest Iowa DNR Water Summary Update. [Iowa DNR](https://www.iowadnr.gov/newsroom/news-releases)

• Farm combine fire near Sergeant Bluff – A farm combine fire was reported near Sergeant Bluff, Iowa. [KTIV](https://www.ktiv.com)

• Tractor destroyed in fire southeast of Lake View – A tractor was destroyed in a fire southeast of Lake View, Iowa. [KTIV](https://www.ktiv.com)

## More for Greg

• Weekend weather for Lawton, Iowa – The National Weather Service forecasts sunny skies for Lawton with today's high near 49°F and overnight low near 32°F. Monday looks sunny with a high near 54°F. Rain is possible later in the week, with a 70% chance Friday and 90% Friday night. [National Weather Service](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lat=42.4697&lon=-96.2551)

• Chiefs vs. Falcons in Week 11 – The Kansas City Chiefs (6-2, 2nd in AFC West) face the Atlanta Falcons. Patrick Mahomes leads the Chiefs with 2,037 passing yards and 14 touchdowns. Kenneth Walker III (groin) is expected to miss the game. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/team/_/name/kc/kansas-city-chiefs)
