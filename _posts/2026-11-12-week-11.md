## Summary

Week 11 byes hit several teams Greg may roster, and a fresh batch of injuries opens waiver-wire moves, while Siouxland enjoys sunny Veterans Day weather and local dining and school news.

## Fantasy Football (Week 11)

• Week 11 byes – Atlanta, Cleveland, Green Bay, the Los Angeles Rams, New England, and Seattle are all on bye this week. If Greg has players from those teams, he needs fill-ins for his lineup. [ESPN Schedule](https://www.espn.com/nfl/schedule/_/week/11)

• Jaylen Wright (RB, Dolphins) – Out – Miami rookie running back Jaylen Wright is ruled out, cutting into the Dolphins backfield with DeVon Achane and Malik Willis already on injured reserve. Greg should look for a replacement running back. [ESPN Injuries](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Kyler Murray (QB, Cardinals) – Doubtful – Kyler Murray (ankle) is listed as doubtful for Week 11, making his start unlikely and leaving the Cardinals quarterback situation uncertain. [ESPN Injuries](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Cole Kmet (TE, Bears) – Injured Reserve – Bears tight end Cole Kmet is on injured reserve, thinning Chicago at tight end for fantasy managers who rostered him. [ESPN Injuries](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Garrett Wilson (WR, Jets) – Questionable – Jets receiver Garrett Wilson is listed as questionable. Greg should monitor his status before locking him in as a starter. [ESPN Injuries](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Waiver-wire adds – Caleb Douglas and Fernando Mendoza are among the top free-agent options, with Aaron Rodgers also available in some leagues, per ESPN's latest waiver report. Good targets for Greg to replace bye-week and injured players. ## Around Siouxland & Iowa

• Da Kao restaurant reopens in Sioux City – Da Kao, a Vietnamese and Chinese restaurant on West 7th Street, reopened its dining room Wednesday, Nov. 11, after finishing repairs following an August crash. [KTIV](https://www.ktiv.com)

• Loess Hills Elementary flag-folding ceremony – On Wednesday, Nov. 11, students at Loess Hills Elementary got firsthand experience in the traditional folding of the American flag for Veterans Day. [KTIV](https://www.ktiv.com)

• Woodbury County post-election audit – Woodbury County officials hand-counted nearly 2,000 ballots on Tuesday, Nov. 10, as part of a post-election audit. [KTIV](https://www.ktiv.com)

• Siouxland weather – Sunny and cold today with a high near 44°F in the Sioux City area, dropping to the 20s overnight. Clear skies through the weekend. [National Weather Service](https://api.weather.gov/gridpoints/FSD/112,16/forecast)
