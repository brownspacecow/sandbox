## Summary

Week 10 features key fantasy football injury updates, waiver wire adds, and start/sit recommendations, including the Jessie Bates III trade from the Falcons. Iowa DNR news releases, local Siouxland updates, and Midwest weather round out the day.

## Fantasy Football (Week 10)

• Corey Kiner could lead Patriots backfield vs. Lions – Kiner (31% rostered) could see a larger role with Rhamondre Stevenson questionable (knee tendon) and TreVeyon Henderson out. He rushed for 55 yards and a TD on 8 carries vs. the Packers last week and leads New England with 4.9 yards per carry. Detroit allows the second-fewest fantasy points per game to RBs, so it is a tough matchup, but Kiner could see 14-plus rushing attempts. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/50453290/week-10-predictions-upsets-fantasy-sleepers-stats-2026)

• Tutu Atwell in line for more Rams targets – With Puka Nacua and Davante Adams both sidelined (IR), Atwell could see significantly more targets against the Cardinals. He has drawn 9 targets over his past two games and played more snaps than any other healthy Rams receiver against the Commanders. Arizona allows 32.2 fantasy points per game to WRs. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/50453290/week-10-predictions-upsets-fantasy-sleepers-stats-2026)

• Jadarian Price predicted to score first NFL TD – Price had 78 total yards and 4 receptions for 54 yards in the Week 9 win over the Cardinals. He will split touches with Zach Charbonnet against the Raiders but is projected to score on a screen pass. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/50453290/week-10-predictions-upsets-fantasy-sleepers-stats-2026)

• George Kittle strong start/sit recommendation – Kittle has seen at least 7 targets in three straight games and faces a prime matchup versus the Cowboys defense. The 49ers lead the league in red zone touchdown percentage (84.6%). Expect multiple red zone targets. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/50453290/week-10-predictions-upsets-fantasy-sleepers-stats-2026)

• Chuba Hubbard could find the end zone vs. Saints – Hubbard has 20+ touches in 4 contests and at least one rushing score in 3 of his past 5 games. The Saints allow 133.9 rushing yards per game (4th most) and 11 rushing TDs (tied 2nd most). [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/50453290/week-10-predictions-upsets-fantasy-sleepers-stats-2026)

• Jessie Bates III traded by Falcons – Atlanta traded away Jessie Bates III and Ryan Neuzil ahead of the deadline. The Falcons insist winning is still the goal despite the roster changes. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/50473666/atlanta-falcons-trade-deadline-jessie-bates-morale)

• Multiple injuries and IR designations – Key players out or on IR include Dyami Brown (WR, calf, IR Nov 7), Daron Payne (DT, foot, season-ending surgery Nov 4), Jeremy McNichols (RB, foot, out for season Nov 4), Javon Kinlaw (DT, MCL surgery Nov 4), Terry McLaurin (WR, knee surgery Oct 30, season-ending), and Lucas Patrick (G, concussion, out for Thursday game). [ESPN Injuries](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries/_/season/2026/week/10)
