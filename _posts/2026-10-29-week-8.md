## Summary

Week 8 of the fantasy football season opens Thursday with key injury calls on Lamar Jackson, DeVonta Smith, and A.J. Brown, plus a rainy Halloween weekend outlook and a $292 million bid to rebuild Sioux City's wastewater treatment plant.

## Fantasy Football (Week 8)

• Lamar Jackson expected back for Ravens-Bills – Jackson (ankle) missed the past three games with a high-ankle sprain but was practicing Wednesday and is expected to play Week 8 against Buffalo. His return boosts Derrick Henry, Mark Andrews, and the whole Baltimore offense. [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/fantasy/article/nfl-injury-report-week-8-latest-news-updates-on-lamar-jackson-breece-hall-more-players-for-fantasy-football-170704038.html)

• DeVonta Smith a game-time decision – Smith (hamstring) did not practice Thursday and has missed three of the past four games. If he sits, rookie Makai Lemon is a solid WR3/flex option in PPR formats. [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/fantasy/article/nfl-injury-report-week-8-latest-news-updates-on-lamar-jackson-breece-hall-more-players-for-fantasy-football-170704038.html)

• Breece Hall (quad) among RBs to watch – Hall is listed on the Week 8 injury report with a quad injury; his status for the Jets should be monitored through the weekend. [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/fantasy/article/nfl-injury-report-week-8-latest-news-updates-on-lamar-jackson-breece-hall-more-players-for-fantasy-football-170704038.html)

• Rashee Rice returns to practice – Rice (hamstring) was back in a limited fashion Wednesday for the Chiefs, though he's only a WR3/flex option if active. [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/fantasy/article/nfl-injury-report-week-8-latest-news-updates-on-lamar-jackson-breece-hall-more-players-for-fantasy-football-170704038.html)

• Set & Forget: Rhamondre Stevenson vs. Dolphins – Stevenson has topped 17 fantasy points in three of his last four games and faces a Miami defense that has allowed 100+ rush yards to a running back three straight weeks. [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/fantasy/article/fantasy-football-start-em-sit-em-joel-smyth-helps-with-tough-flex-lineup-decisions-for-week-8-145428348.html)

• Waiver-wire adds – Makai Lemon (Commanders WR, 44% rostered), Chig Okonkwo (Commanders TE, 27%), and Mike Gesicki (Titans TE, 16%) are under-the-radar adds for Week 8. [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/fantasy/article/week-8-fantasy-football-sleepers-2-rookie-receivers-stick-out-as-underrated-options-off-the-waiver-wire-141933244.html)

## Around Siouxland & Iowa

• Rain chances through Halloween weekend – Showers develop late Thursday and linger into the weekend, with mostly cloudy skies and highs in the middle 60s on Halloween. Remember to set clocks back an hour Saturday night for daylight saving time. [KTIV](https://www.ktiv.com/2026/10/29/rain-chances-expected-throughout-halloween-weekend/)

• $292M bid for Sioux City wastewater plant – PKG Contracting of North Dakota submitted the lowest of three bids, at $292 million, to rebuild the Sioux City wastewater treatment plant. The City Council still must vote on it. [KTIV](https://www.ktiv.com/2026/10/29/p-k-g-contracting-submits-lowest-bid-sioux-city-wastewater-plant-project/)

• Eddie's Tavern raises money for Boys & Girls Clubs – Guest bartenders at Eddie's Tavern in Sergeant Bluff raised money for the Boys & Girls Clubs of Siouxland. [KTIV](https://www.ktiv.com/2026/10/29/eddies-tavern-raises-money-siouxland-boys-girls-clubs-through-guest-bartenders/)

• Iowa State Kid Captain in remission – 6-year-old Kaisley King, who lives near Boone, is this week's Iowa State football Kid Captain and is now in remission from a rare immune disorder. [KTIV](https://www.ktiv.com/2026/10/29/iowa-state-kid-captain-now-remission-rare-immune-disorder/)
