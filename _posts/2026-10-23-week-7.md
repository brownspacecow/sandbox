## Summary

Cardinals rookie RB Jeremiyah Love will miss a second straight game with an ankle injury, and Terry McLaurin is out for the season — both big for Week 7 fantasy lineups. Siouxland stays mild through the weekend before a cooldown Sunday.

## Fantasy Football (Week 7)

• Cardinals RB Jeremiyah Love to miss second straight game – Love (ankle) will not play Sunday against the Broncos, per Cardinals beat reporter Darren Urban. This is Love's second missed game in a row, so fantasy managers should adjust their Cardinals running back expectations accordingly. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/50259624/cardinals-rookie-rb-jeremiyah-love-miss-second-straight-game)

• Terry McLaurin out for the season – Washington Commanders wide receiver Terry McLaurin is done for the year, per ESPN injury data. Fantasy managers should move on from McLaurin and look for waiver wire replacements at wide receiver. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries/_/week/7)

• Texans LT Laremy Tunsil on injured reserve – Houston left tackle Laremy Tunsil has been placed on injured reserve. This impacts the Texans offensive line and could affect quarterback protection and the run game. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries/_/week/7)

• DraftKings DFS values for Week 7 main slate – ESPN's DraftKings DFS cheat sheet identifies the best value plays for Sunday's games. Good starting point for finding underpriced players before setting lineups. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/fantasy/football/story/_/id/50259638/draftkings-nfl-dfs-values-week-7)

• Cardinals kicking situation uncertain – Kickers Jon Gaines II (out) and others dealing with designations. Check the Cardinals special teams situation before relying on any Arizona kicker this week. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries/_/week/7)

## Around Siouxland & Iowa

• Siouxland 7-day forecast – National Weather Service calls for mostly sunny skies today with a high near 77°F and breezy south winds 15-20 mph. Saturday mostly sunny then a slight chance of showers with a high near 73°F. Sunday cools down with a high near 63°F and mostly cloudy skies. [National Weather Service](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lat=42.48&lon=-95.87)
