## Summary

Week 9 injury news spotlights Cardinals offensive linemen Jayden Williams and Jon Gaines II listed questionable, James Conner out on injured reserve, while Ravens quarterback Lamar Jackson joins injured reserve. Siouxland enjoys a crisp, sunny weekend ahead.

## Fantasy Football (Week 9)

• Jayden Williams (Cardinals OT) – questionable (knee) – Cardinals tackle Jayden Williams (knee) did not practice Thursday and is listed questionable for Week 9, per ESPN citing Cardinals beat reporter Darren Urban. Fantasy managers should monitor his status before Sunday. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Jon Gaines II (Cardinals G) – questionable (ankle) – Guard Jon Gaines II (ankle) also sat out Thursday practice and is questionable, per ESPN citing Darren Urban. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• James Conner (Cardinals RB) – out on injured reserve – Running back James Conner (foot) remains on injured reserve and will not return from IR this week, per ESPN citing Darren Urban of the Cardinals' official site. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Lamar Jackson (Ravens QB) – placed on injured reserve – Ravens quarterback Lamar Jackson (thumb) has been placed on injured reserve, per ESPN and the Ravens' official site. His season is effectively over. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Kyle Hamilton (Ravens S) – questionable (hip) – Ravens safety Kyle Hamilton (hip) is questionable and was ruled out for Thursday night's game, per ESPN citing the Ravens' official site. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)
