## Around Siouxland & Iowa

• Le Mars American Legion hosts 3rd annual pancake breakfast fundraiser – Community members gathered Sunday, Oct. 24, for the third annual pancake breakfast supporting Charlie Troop 1-113 Cavalry Regiment and the Soldier and Family Readiness Group. The event brings the community together to show appreciation for soldiers and their families, with funds going to other Legion events throughout the year. [KTIV](https://www.ktiv.com/2026/10/25/le-mars-american-legion-host-3rd-annual-pancake-breakfast-fundraiser/)
• Siouxland Soup Kitchen announces major expansion – The soup kitchen at 717 W. 7th Street plans to add about 1,500 square feet of new dining space. The $1.1 million project is already 75% funded, including a $225,000 impact match grant. Meals served have risen from 26,293 in 2022 to 39,894 in 2025, with 28,274 meals already served through September 2026. Construction expected to begin next spring, targeting completion by October 2027. [KSCJ 1360](https://kscj.com/2026/10/25/siouxland-soup-kitchen-announces-major-expansion/)
• Sioux City to host Morningside Avenue corridor study meeting – The city's second public meeting on the Morningside Avenue Corridor Study takes place Tuesday afternoon from 4PM to 5:30PM at the Morningside Branch Library, 4005 Morningside Avenue. The planning team will present preliminary corridor concepts shaped by public suggestions and technical findings. [KSCJ 1360](https://kscj.com/2026/10/25/sioux-city-to-host-morningside-avenue-corridor-study-meeting/)
• Sioux City forecast: Rain chances ahead with steady warming trend – A cool, rainy Sunday with showers possible through the day and into next week. Above-average temperatures are expected, with highs in the low 70s possible by Thursday. Rain is possible on Halloween. [KTIV](https://www.ktiv.com/2026/10/25/plenty-rain-chances-ahead-along-with-steady-warming-trend-likely/)
