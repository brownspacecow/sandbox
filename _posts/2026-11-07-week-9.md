## Summary

Week 9 injury news spotlights new Chiefs and Jets status changes ahead of Sunday's Arrowhead showdown, while Siouxland prepares for Veterans Day with a crisp, sunny weekend forecast.

## Fantasy Football (Week 9)

• Nikko Remigio (Chiefs WR) – questionable (hip) – Remigio (hip) has been deemed questionable to suit up against the Jets on Sunday, per ESPN injury reports and confirmed on NFL.com's official injury report. Chiefs fantasy managers should monitor his status before kickoff. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Mansoor Delane (Chiefs CB) – out (shoulder) – Chiefs head coach Andy Reid said Friday that Delane (shoulder) will not play Sunday against the Jets, per ESPN and NFL.com. He was listed as out on both reports. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Omarr Norman-Lott (Chiefs DT) – designated to return from injured reserve (knee) – The Chiefs designated Norman-Lott (knee) to return from injured reserve Wednesday, per ESPN. Note: NFL.com still lists him as out, so his status is not yet confirmed for Sunday. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Garrett Wilson (Jets WR) – out (concussion) – Wilson (concussion) is out for Sunday's game against the Chiefs, per ESPN citing Brian Costello of the New York Post, and confirmed as out on NFL.com's injury report. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Trevin Wallace (Jets LB) – out (groin) – Jets head coach Aaron Glenn said Friday that Wallace (groin) will not play in Sunday's matchup against the Chiefs, per ESPN and confirmed on NFL.com. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Hunter Henry (Patriots TE) – questionable (foot) – Henry (foot) is listed as questionable for Sunday's game against the Packers, per ESPN citing Doug Kyed of the Boston Herald, and NFL.com shows him as limited in practice with a questionable game status. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

## Around Siouxland & Iowa

• Veterans Day events in Siouxland – Siouxland businesses and organizations will host events on Wednesday, Nov. 11, 2026, to honor those who've served and who are currently serving in the armed forces. [KTIV](https://www.ktiv.com/)

• Siouxland Sleep Out – The community came together to raise awareness and support for homelessness. Sioux City experienced a 63% increase in unsheltered individuals between 2025 and 2026, per the Sioux City Point in Time Count. [KTIV](https://www.ktiv.com/)

• Hawarden Holiday Express tickets on sale – The Hawarden Holiday Express rolls out on December 5, but seats go on sale this Saturday, November 7. [KTIV](https://www.ktiv.com/)

• State Fire Marshal carbon monoxide warning – With temperatures dropping and winter approaching, the Iowa State Fire Marshal Division issued a reminder urging residents to take precautions against carbon monoxide poisoning. [KTIV/KCRG](https://www.ktiv.com/)
