## Summary

Week 12 of the NFL season is here, and fantasy managers face some big decisions with key running backs and quarterbacks sidelined. Meanwhile, Siouxland is getting into the holiday spirit with Le Mars' Christmas celebration and a local food drive.

## Fantasy Football (Week 12)

• James Conner (Cardinals RB) placed on injured reserve – The Arizona workhorse running back is out with a foot injury and missed Week 12. He's been a dependable fantasy starter, so owners need to scramble for a replacement at running back. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Gardner Minshew (Cardinals QB) out for the season – Minshew underwent UCL reconstructive surgery on his elbow and is done for 2026. This leaves Arizona thin at quarterback and affects any offensive weapon fantasy value. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Carson Beck (Commanders QB) out for Sunday – The Commanders quarterback will not play against Washington with a back injury, per coach Mike LaFleur. If you roster Commanders skill players, expect a downgrade in production. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Jon Gaines II (Commanders OL) out – Gaines is out with an ankle injury for Sunday's game versus Washington, adding to the Commanders' offensive line concerns. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Hunter Long (Commanders TE) questionable – Long is questionable with an ankle injury for Sunday's matchup. A game-time decision to monitor before setting lineups. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)
