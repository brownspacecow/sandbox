## Summary

Fantasy managers head into Week 11 with several key injuries to track, including New England's Drake Maye out for the season and Seattle's Kenneth Walker III sidelined for this week. The bye-week drain hits six teams, including Green Bay and Seattle, leaving managers scrambling for fill-ins.

## Fantasy Football (Week 11)

• Drake Maye out for the season – The Patriots quarterback fractured his right fibula and will miss the rest of the year. Fantasy managers at quarterback need a fill-in plan for the final stretch. [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/nfl/) - cross-checked with [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Kenneth Walker III out for Week 11 – The Seahawks running back is sidelined with a groin injury this week. Fantasy owners should plan for a replacement at running back for Sunday's slate. [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/nfl/) - cross-checked with [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Carson Beck dealing with back spasms – The Cardinals quarterback is limited in practice with back spasms, and Arizona is preparing for Josh Johnson to start. Managers should treat Beck as doubtful until confirmed. [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/nfl/) - cross-checked with [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Chiefs add WR Josh Downs via trade – Kansas City traded for Josh Downs on Tuesday, giving Patrick Mahomes a new target heading into the Cardinals game. Downs is worth a look as a waiver add in deeper leagues. [Chiefs.com](https://www.chiefs.com/news)

• Week 11 byes – Atlanta, Cleveland, Green Bay, Los Angeles Rams, New England, and Seattle are on bye. Managers with players from those teams need full fill-in lineups. [NFL.com schedule](https://www.nfl.com/schedule/2026/REG11)

• Sioux City weekend forecast – Saturday will be mostly cloudy with a high near 46 and northwest winds 10 to 15 mph. Sunday looks sunny with a high near 39. Bundle up if you're headed outdoors. [National Weather Service](https://api.weather.gov/gridpoints/FSD/56,72/forecast)
