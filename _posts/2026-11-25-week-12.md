## Summary

Week 12 fantasy football injury and suspension news, plus cold Thanksgiving weather across Siouxland and the Chiefs' loss to the Lions on Sunday.

## Fantasy Football (Week 12)

• Jeremiyah Love (Cardinals RB) – placed on injured reserve with an ankle injury – The Cardinals running back was placed on IR Monday, with the team still evaluating his status. If you roster Love, you'll need a replacement for Week 12. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Zachariah Branch (Falcons WR) – listed questionable with a hamstring – Branch went through warmups Monday but remains a game-time decision for Week 12. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Sam Darnold (Seahawks QB) – questionable with a quadriceps/rib injury – Seattle's quarterback made "a good bit of progress" this week but is still listed as questionable. Monitor his status before finalizing your lineup. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Christian Benford (Bills CB) – questionable with a groin injury – The Buffalo Bills cornerback was limited in Tuesday's walkthrough practice. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• Dalton Kincaid (Bills TE) – questionable with a thumb/ankle injury – The Bills tight end was listed as limited in Tuesday's practice. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/injuries)

• J.K. Dobbins (Broncos RB) – suspended one game – The Denver Broncos running back is suspended for conduct detrimental to the team. Tyler Badie was designated to return from IR and could see added snaps. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/transactions)

• George Holani (Seahawks RB) – placed on injured reserve with a knee injury – The Seattle Seahawks running back was moved to IR Monday, opening a spot in the backfield. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/transactions)
