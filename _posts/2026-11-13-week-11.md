## Summary

Siouxland enjoys sunny, cool Veterans Day weekend weather, with local high school football teams advancing to state championships and a new data center coming to Estherville.

## Around Siouxland & Iowa

• PathOne Data Centers held an information meeting in Estherville, Iowa, on Friday, Nov. 13, about a proposed data center project. [KCAU9](https://www.kcau9.com/news/local-news/pathone-data-centers-holds-information-meeting-in-estherville/)

• Sergeant Bluff-Luton football punched its ticket to the IHSAA 4A state championship game with a 28-15 win over Carlisle on Friday, Nov. 13. [KCAU9](https://www.kcau9.com/sports/sergeant-bluff-luton-football-punches-ticket-to-ihsaa-4a-state-championship-game-with-28-15-win-over-carlisle/)

• Woodbury Central football marched into the IHSAA Class A championship game with a 43-14 win over Maquoketa Valley on Thursday, Nov. 12. [KCAU9](https://www.kcau9.com/sports/woodbury-central-football-marches-into-ihsaa-class-a-championship-game-with-43-14-win-over-maquoketa-valley/)

• West Sioux won the Class A semifinal 52-21 over North Linn on Thursday, Nov. 12, with Demke snagging four interceptions and Lopez rushing for four touchdowns. [KCAU9](https://www.kcau9.com/sports/demke-snags-four-interceptions-on-defense-lopez-rushes-for-four-touchdowns-in-west-siouxs-52-21-win-over-north-linn-in-class-a-semifinal/)

• Siouxland had sunny and cool weather Friday with a high near 43F in the Sioux City area, dropping into the 20s overnight. Mostly sunny through the weekend with highs in the mid-40s to upper 40s; a chance of light rain arrives Wednesday night into Thursday. [National Weather Service](https://api.weather.gov/gridpoints/FSD/108,16/forecast)

• South Sioux City community members provided input on a local trail project on Friday, Nov. 13. [KCAU9](https://www.kcau9.com/news/local-news/south-sioux-city-community-members-provide-input-on-trail-project/)

## More for Greg
