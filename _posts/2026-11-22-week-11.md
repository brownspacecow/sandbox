## Summary

Puka Nacua is out for the season after MCL surgery, the Colts are turning to Anthony Richardson at quarterback, and Sioux City expects sunny but cold weather for Sunday's slate.

## Fantasy Football (Week 11)

• Puka Nacua out for the season – The Rams wide receiver underwent MCL surgery and faces a four-to-five-month recovery, meaning he will miss the playoffs and the rest of the 2026 season. Fantasy managers should move on from Nacua and look for a replacement wide receiver. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/50556613/sources-rams-puka-nacua-4-5-months-knee-surgery)

• Roman Wilson activated off IR – The Steelers activated wide receiver Roman Wilson from injured reserve ahead of Sunday's game against the Eagles, giving Pittsburgh's passing game a boost alongside DK Metcalf and Michael Pittman Jr. Wilson is worth a waiver look in deeper leagues. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/50552701/sources-steelers-activate-wr-roman-wilson-ir-eagles-game)

• Colts turn to Anthony Richardson – Indianapolis cut quarterback Daniel Jones and will start Anthony Richardson going forward. Richardson is an immediate waiver add at quarterback in most formats and a stash in deeper leagues. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/50542293/colts-release-qb-daniel-jones-blowout-loss-texans)

• Week 11 start/sit and matchups – ESPN's lineup advice breaks down the vital insights for Sunday's slate, including which Titans and Broncos players have opportunity this week. Check this before finalizing your starting lineup. [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/fantasy/football/story/_/id/50531472/fantasy-football-start-sit-week-11-need-know)

## Around Siouxland & Iowa

• Sioux City forecast for Sunday, November 22 – Sunny with a high near 41 and temperatures falling into the afternoon; northwest winds 10 to 15 mph with gusts up to 25 mph. Bundle up if you are headed outdoors. [National Weather Service](https://api.weather.gov/gridpoints/FSD/112,15/forecast)
