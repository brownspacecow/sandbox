## Around Siouxland & Iowa

• Deere bringing back 275 Waterloo workers – John Deere is recalling 275 laid-off workers to its Waterloo, Iowa facility and plans to hire hundreds more. The farm equipment manufacturer is adding jobs in eastern Iowa. [KTIV](https://www.ktiv.com/2026/10/30/deere-bringing-back-275-waterloo-workers-hiring-hundreds-more/)

• Showers and drizzle moving into Siouxland for Halloween – Scattered showers are expected Friday with more rain possible on Halloween. Trick-or-treaters may want to plan for wet weather. [KTIV](https://www.ktiv.com/2026/10/30/isolated-showers-drizzle-moving-into-siouxland-halloween/)
